# Christmas Village 0.8.0

Five new discoveries at the Christmas table:
- Wipe frost from the window; its wooden frame and foreground lantern stay clear.
- Touch the window garland for a gentle trail of warm lights.
- Warm the little window lantern.
- Watch delicate steam rise from the Christmas roast.
- Catch candlelight on the glossy cranberries.

Living-room and table wipe marks are independent and survive room visits.
All effects share the existing animation loop. Reduced motion keeps highlights
steady and supports wiping through touch or keyboard.

Validation: 24 desktop/mobile Chromium tests, web build and offline iOS bundle checks.
Native iOS/Safari execution remains unverified on Windows.
